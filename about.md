I'm Jakub Milkiewicz, a software engineer and hands-on architect based between
Poznań and Berlin. I've been building backend systems for almost 20 years:
from banking in Poland, through eBay and SAP, to a long run of Berlin startups
and scale-ups.

Some of the things I've built along the way:

- **Workflow orchestration with Temporal**: as Tech Lead at Orcrist, a platform
  that processes customer audio, video, images and chat exports through
  configurable, LLM-powered pipelines (TypeScript workflows, Python activities).
- **Real-time bidding**: a DSP serving thousands of bids per second at Webtrekk,
  and later the discovery and architecture of a new RTB platform
  for a major Polish e-commerce company.
- **ML-driven products**: bandit algorithms optimizing ad revenue at eBay Kleinanzeigen.
- **Fintech**: fully automated loan risk analysis integrated with SCHUFA
  and decision engines at Lendico.
- **Data pipelines**: streaming and batch on Kafka, Spark, Beam and Debezium.

I mostly work in Kotlin, Java and TypeScript, usually as a hands-on architect
who designs the system and writes much of the code. My toolbox includes
DDD and Event Storming for modelling, event-driven architecture on Kafka,
and TDD plus explicit contracts between services, so that a system
stays easy to change long after the first release.<br/>
I co-organized GeeCON and was active in the Poznań JUG and
Software Craftsmanship Berlin, so sharing what I learn comes naturally.
That's what this blog is for.

**Open to new opportunities**: Tech Lead / Staff / hands-on architect roles
or freelance projects, remote or in Poland/Germany. Especially interested
in durable execution, distributed workflows and LLM-based systems.
